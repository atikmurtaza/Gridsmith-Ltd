## 1. Summary

We use one cookie to remember your cookie choice. Optional analytics, Google Analytics 4 and PostHog, can run only if you allow them, and you can allow or refuse each one separately. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting, heatmaps or session recording.

The cookie request on the website lists the optional analytics services that are switched on for it. A service that is not listed there is not in use, and nothing is set for it. While no optional service is switched on, the website shows a cookie notice instead, and the button at the foot of our pages that reopens it is labelled Cookie notice.

The gs_consent cookie is set only when you respond to our cookie notice or request. It is not an analytics identifier and does not permit tracking. It needs no consent because it is used only to remember your cookie choice or, where no optional analytics are offered, that you have seen our cookie notice.

Our own code does not use local storage, session storage or IndexedDB to store information about you. If you allow PostHog, it uses the session storage entries above, which your browser deletes when you close the tab. When you withdraw consent, we remove the analytics cookies and PostHog's browser storage from your device. The public website has no sign-in or payment checkout. Our website hosting and content management providers still process technical request information, as the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains.

## 3. Optional analytics

Optional analytics run only on a version of our website whose cookie request offers them. When they are offered and you allow them, we use:

- **Google Analytics 4**, provided by Google, to count the pages visited and how visitors arrive at the website, as statistics; and
- **PostHog**, provided by PostHog using its European Union hosting, to count the pages visited and how visitors arrive at the website, as statistics.

We use these statistics to understand how the website is used and to improve its pages and contact journeys. We use PostHog to record page views. Google Analytics records page views and standard first-visit, session and engagement information. We use neither service for advertising or personalised-advertising features, Google signals, session recording, surveys, heatmaps or automatic capture of what you click, scroll or type, and we do not combine them with other information to identify you. Page addresses are sent without any query or fragment. When an analytics service loads, its provider receives your browser's technical information, including your IP address. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains what each provider receives, where it is processed and how long it is kept.

Each service stays off until you choose to allow it. Choosing Reject optional, or making no choice, keeps both off. The website works in the same way whatever you choose, and our contact links remain available.

## 4. Your choices

Before any optional analytics can run, we ask you to accept all, reject optional analytics, or customise your choice for each service. Nothing is selected in advance.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Cookie settings button at the foot of our pages. Withdrawing consent stops further collection straight away in every page of our website open in that browser, and removes the analytics cookies and browser storage from your device. It does not erase information a provider has already received; the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains how to ask us about access or erasure.

We ask again after 180 days, or sooner if we add a service or change what the services are used for.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you delete the gs_consent cookie, we will ask again. Without JavaScript, the cookie request does not appear and no optional analytics can run.
