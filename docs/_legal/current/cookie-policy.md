## 1. Current use

We use a cookie to remember acknowledgement of our cookie notice. GA4, PostHog and AI-assisted analytics are planned future processing and are not active. We do not currently use advertising cookies, retargeting, heatmaps or session recording.

## 2. Cookie we use

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|---|---|---|---|
| gs_consent | Remembers that you selected Got it on the cookie notice | Up to 365 days | Gridsmith Ltd |

This cookie is set only when you acknowledge the notice. It is not an analytics identifier or permission to track you. It remembers your requested preference under the applicable preference or strictly necessary exception in the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

Our application does not use local storage, session storage or IndexedDB to track you. The public website has no sign-in or payment checkout. Hosting and content services still process technical request information, as the Privacy Policy explains.

## 3. Your choices

The footer's **Cookie notice** link reopens the notice. You can remove the acknowledgement cookie through your browser settings. Browser settings also let you block cookies; this may cause the notice to reappear.

## 4. Future analytics

We plan to use Google Analytics 4 and PostHog to understand website use and contact journeys, with your consent. Before activation, we will update this policy with the actual cookies, purposes, providers and durations, and offer an equally prominent choice to accept or reject analytics. Enquiries will remain available if you reject. You will be able to withdraw consent through Cookie preferences.

Withdrawing consent stops further consent-based collection. Removing a cookie does not itself erase information already received by a provider. Contact us about access or erasure; our Privacy Policy explains your rights and our planned use of AI-assisted report analysis.

## 5. External links and content
