## 1. Our approach

We want gridsmith.uk to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people who use keyboard navigation, screen readers, magnification or reduced-motion settings.

We treat accessibility as part of the design and development of the website.

## 2. Current status

Our design and development target is **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**. This target does not limit our legal duty to make reasonable adjustments.

We are **not currently claiming formal full conformance** with WCAG 2.2 AA because a comprehensive assistive-technology and screen-reader audit across every public route has not been completed.

## 3. Measures we take

Our development process includes measures such as:

- semantic page structure;
- keyboard accessibility;
- visible focus states;
- text and interface contrast checks;
- responsive layouts;
- reduced-motion consideration; and
- automated accessibility checks on selected routes.

Automated testing does not detect every accessibility problem, so it is not treated as proof of full conformance.

## 4. Known limitations

Some routes or components have received less manual or assistive-technology testing than others.

Contact us by email at contact@gridsmith.uk, or through the WhatsApp link on our contact pages. The website does not use enquiry forms. These contact links do not require website JavaScript. Email remains available if WhatsApp is unavailable or inaccessible; we do not claim that external email or messaging services are fully accessible.

If we identify an accessibility barrier, we will assess it promptly, make reasonable adjustments and offer an effective alternative where appropriate while a correction is prepared. We do not charge for a reasonable adjustment. Accessibility requests are considered on their circumstances rather than postponed automatically to a general development schedule.

## 5. Requesting an adjustment or reporting a problem

If you cannot access information or use a feature of the website, email **contact@gridsmith.uk** and tell us:

- which page or feature caused the problem;
- what you were trying to do; and
- any format or adjustment that would help.

We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable and discuss a suitable adjustment or alternative with you.

## 6. Alternative formats

Where reasonably possible, we can provide important information in an alternative accessible format or through another reasonable method.

## 7. Legal position

Nothing in this statement limits duties that apply to Gridsmith Ltd under the Equality Act 2010 or other applicable law.

## 8. Review

We will review this statement as the site develops and after significant accessibility testing or redesign work.
