## 1. Our approach

We want gridsmith.uk to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people who use keyboard navigation, screen readers, magnification or reduced-motion settings.

We treat accessibility as part of the design and development of the website.

## 2. Standard and current status

We test the website against the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA**, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. This target does not limit our legal duty to make reasonable adjustments.

We are **not claiming full conformance** with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Our October 2026 testing, described in section 3, found two problems, which we corrected before this statement was prepared, and no others in the checks it covers. Those checks cannot confirm every requirement on their own, and the testing in section 4 has not yet been carried out.

## 3. How we tested

On 28 and 29 October 2026 we tested every page of a pre-release version of the website, except its error pages, in a desktop browser (Google Chrome), against the Level A and Level AA success criteria of WCAG 2.2:

- automated accessibility rules at four screen widths, from 1440 down to 320 CSS pixels, with the cookie notice closed, and at 1440 pixels with it showing;
- reflow at a 320 CSS pixel width, which is equivalent to 400% zoom; increased text spacing; and the size of buttons and links;
- keyboard-only navigation of every page, checking that focus is visible, is not entirely hidden by fixed parts of the page and is never trapped;
- page structure, headings, landmarks, page titles, page language and the skip link;
- every page with JavaScript turned off and with a reduced-motion setting, and whether anything on the page keeps moving more than five seconds after it loads;
- the cookie notice and cookie settings, including keyboard use and small screens.

Where automated tools could not determine a colour contrast, for text over the animated scenes on the home, Design, Digital and Press pages or beneath a sticky bar, we measured the rendered colours with separate scripted checks.

The testing found two problems, and we corrected both before this statement was prepared:

- keyboard focus was not visible on the header and footer links of the 13 Design service pages;
- the animated scenes on the home, Design and Digital pages kept moving with no way to pause them (WCAG 2.2 success criterion 2.2.2, Pause, Stop, Hide).

## 4. What we have not yet tested

We have not yet tested the website with screen readers (such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack), speech recognition or switch access, on physical phones and tablets, with operating-system high-contrast or forced-colour modes, or with disabled users. We have not yet reviewed by hand whether the focus order, link text, headings and descriptions are logical and meaningful. Automated and scripted testing does not detect every accessibility problem, so it is not treated as proof of conformance.

## 5. Known limitations

The animated scenes on the home, Design, Digital and Press pages are decorative illustrations of content that is also given as text, and they follow your device's reduced-motion setting. On the home page, the slow sway of the mark comes to rest within five seconds of the page loading or of your last scroll. The Design page has a Pause motion button in its opening section, which shows the page without animation. On the Digital page the compass moves only during its opening and closing sequences, which the Pause motion button stops. The Press page has a Pause the sequence button.

Contact us by email at [contact@gridsmith.uk](mailto:contact@gridsmith.uk), or through the WhatsApp link on our [contact pages](/contact). The website does not use enquiry forms. These contact links do not require website JavaScript. Email remains available if WhatsApp is unavailable or inaccessible; we do not claim that external email or messaging services are fully accessible.

If we identify an accessibility barrier, we will assess it promptly, make reasonable adjustments and offer an effective alternative where appropriate while a correction is prepared. We do not charge for a reasonable adjustment. Accessibility requests are considered on their circumstances rather than postponed automatically to a general development schedule.

## 6. Requesting an adjustment or reporting a problem

If you cannot access information or use a feature of the website, email **[contact@gridsmith.uk](mailto:contact@gridsmith.uk)** and tell us:

- which page or feature caused the problem;
- what you were trying to do; and
- any format or adjustment that would help.

We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable and discuss a suitable adjustment or alternative with you.

## 7. Alternative formats

Where reasonably possible, we can provide important information in an alternative accessible format or through another reasonable method.

## 8. Legal position

Nothing in this statement limits duties that apply to Gridsmith Ltd under the Equality Act 2010 or other applicable law.
