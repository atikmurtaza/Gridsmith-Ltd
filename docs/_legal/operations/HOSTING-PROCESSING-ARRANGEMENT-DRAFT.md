# Hosting processing arrangement — SUPERSEDED / NOT EXECUTED

**GS-LEGAL-001-R11, 8 October 2026: SUPERSEDED / NOT EXECUTED.** Do not sign, circulate or
use this draft as compliance evidence. The owner rejects its unsupported assumption that a separate
external account holder is Gridsmith's processor. The owner controls the account; Gridsmith's
authorised internal administrators manage hosting and the mailbox. No external administrator has
been identified. Current Hostinger terms/DPA and the clarified facts do not establish a need for this
additional agreement. Shared personnel do not merge separate legal entities; if a separate entity
actually processes Gridsmith data, assess its real role then. The text below is retained only as
superseded history, including its signature blocks and instructions.

Current authority: `../research/GS-LEGAL-001/R11-PRIVACY-ADOPTION-HOSTING-CLOSEOUT.md`.

**Status:** draft prepared at `GS-LEGAL-001-R10` (8 October 2026) for the owner. **Not signed, not sent,
not in force.** No party has agreed to it. It is not legal advice and has no solicitor review.

**Purpose:** to complete the UK GDPR Art. 28(3) chain for Hostinger's processing of gridsmith.uk data on
the **existing** Hostinger account. It needs no new account, plan, domain, transfer or migration.
Hostinger's DPA §2.2 contemplates exactly this structure:
- its Customer may be "a Processor of the Customer Data";
- that Customer appoints "Hostinger as another Processor";
- the Customer warrants that this has been "authorized by the relevant Controller".

The record of why this is the step is
`../research/GS-LEGAL-001/R10-HOSTING-CLOSEOUT-PRIVACY-PACKAGE.md`.

**Before signing, complete these outside this repository:**

2. **Transfer check:** if the Account Holder is established outside the UK, a UK transfer mechanism

3. **Privacy review:** the executed arrangement may make the Account Holder a recipient. Before

## Agreement

**Parties:**
- **Gridsmith Ltd**, company number 17050842, registered office 30 Briarfield Road, Farnworth, Bolton,
  BL4 0HD (**"Gridsmith"**, the controller).
- **The Account Holder**: the business that is Hostinger's account customer for the hosting account
  through which gridsmith.uk and the contact@gridsmith.uk mailbox are hosted, identified in the executed
  copy (**"the Account Holder"**, the processor).

**1. Scope.** This arrangement covers the personal data processed through the hosting, CDN and email
hosting that Hostinger provides for gridsmith.uk and contact@gridsmith.uk under the Account Holder's
Hostinger account ("**Gridsmith Data**"):
- the content of website requests and their technical logs;
- enquiry-related email and mailbox contents;
- the categories of people described in Gridsmith's Privacy Policy.

It continues for as long as Gridsmith Data is hosted on that account.

**2. Roles.**
- Gridsmith is the controller of Gridsmith Data.
- The Account Holder holds the Hostinger contract for the account. It acts as Gridsmith's processor
  only for Gridsmith Data.
- Gridsmith's admin department manages the gridsmith.uk hosting and mailbox within the account.
- The Account Holder does not use Gridsmith Data for its own purposes.

**3. Instructions.**
- The Account Holder processes Gridsmith Data, and gives Hostinger instructions about it, only on
  Gridsmith's documented instructions. Those are the instructions given through Gridsmith's management
  of the gridsmith.uk hosting and mailbox, or in writing.
- This includes transfers outside the UK: Gridsmith authorises only those made by Hostinger under the
  safeguards in its Data Processing Addendum (§9).
- This applies unless the law requires otherwise, in which case the Account Holder tells Gridsmith first
  unless the law forbids it.
- The Account Holder tells Gridsmith immediately if it considers an instruction unlawful.

**4. Access and confidentiality.**
- The Account Holder does not access Gridsmith Data except where needed to keep the account and its
  services running, on Gridsmith's instructions.
- Anyone it allows to access Gridsmith Data is bound by confidentiality.

**5. Security.**
- The Account Holder keeps the account's access credentials secure and grants or keeps access to the
  gridsmith.uk hosting and mailbox only for people Gridsmith authorises.
- It takes the other measures required by UK GDPR Art. 32 that are within its control as account
  holder.

**6. Hostinger and its sub-processors.**
- Gridsmith authorises the Account Holder to use **Hostinger** as sub-processor for Gridsmith Data,
  under Hostinger's Terms of Service and Data Processing Addendum for the account. That DPA imposes data
  protection obligations on Hostinger and governs Hostinger's own sub-processors (DPA §6).
- This is Gridsmith's general written authorisation under UK GDPR Art. 28(2).
- The Account Holder:
  - forwards to Gridsmith, without undue delay, any notice from Hostinger of a new or replacement
    sub-processor;
  - forwards those notices in time for Gridsmith to object within the period Hostinger's DPA allows;
  - if Gridsmith objects, agrees a response with Gridsmith. Hostinger's remedy is ending the affected
    services, so on a shared account the parties limit any action to the gridsmith.uk services where
    possible.
- The Account Holder engages no other processor for Gridsmith Data without Gridsmith's prior written
  authorisation.
- The Account Holder remains fully liable to Gridsmith for Hostinger's performance of its data protection
  obligations for Gridsmith Data (UK GDPR Art. 28(4)).

**7. Incidents and assistance.**
- The Account Holder forwards to Gridsmith, without undue delay, any security-incident notification
  Hostinger sends to the account's administrators (DPA §7.4) that may concern Gridsmith Data. It keeps
  the account's administrator contact details accurate, and makes sure Gridsmith's admin department
  receives those notifications.
- It helps Gridsmith, taking into account the nature of the processing, with:
  - data-subject requests;
  - Gridsmith's obligations under UK GDPR Arts. 32 to 36.

**8. Continuity and end.**
- The Account Holder does not cancel, suspend or let lapse the services hosting Gridsmith Data, or
  remove Gridsmith's access, without reasonable prior written notice to Gridsmith. The exception is where
  Hostinger or the law requires otherwise.
- When this arrangement ends, the Account Holder, at Gridsmith's choice, enables Gridsmith to export
  Gridsmith Data, or deletes it from the account. It then confirms in writing. Copies required by law are
  excepted.

**9. Information and audit.**
- The Account Holder makes available to Gridsmith the information needed to show compliance with this
  arrangement and UK GDPR Art. 28.
- It allows and contributes to reasonable audits by Gridsmith or an auditor Gridsmith appoints.

**10. Law.** This arrangement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
