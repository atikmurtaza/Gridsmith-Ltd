# Template 04 — Rights and confidentiality schedule: subcontracting company

**Status:** internal template (`GS-LEGAL-001-R4`, 7 October 2026). **Not signed.** Not legal advice.
Fill-ins are `[● …]`.

**Use:** attach to the services agreement with a **company or other organisation** that supplies work
through its own people. Sign it **before any of their work is used**. A company cannot waive its
individuals' moral rights for them (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 ("CDPA") section 87(2)), so
this schedule also obliges it to deliver template 05 from each individual author.

## Schedule — intellectual property and confidentiality

**Parties:** Gridsmith Ltd, company number 17050842 (**"Gridsmith"**), and [● company name], company
number [●], of [● registered office] (**"the Supplier"**).
**Engagement:** [● project reference / description].

### 1. Assignment

1.1 The Supplier **assigns to Gridsmith**, with full title guarantee, all copyright, design right,
database right and other intellectual property rights, in every country, in everything created for the
Engagement by the Supplier or its personnel (the **"Work"**): existing rights now, and future rights by
way of **present assignment of future rights**, so that each vests in Gridsmith on creation (CDPA
sections 90(3) and 91).

1.2 **Not conditional on payment.**

1.3 Pre-existing material: as template 03 clause 1.3, listed in Part A.

### 2. Chain of title

2.1 The Supplier warrants that, before any of them works on the Engagement, every individual who
contributes to the Work is either:
- (a) its **employee**, working in the course of employment, so that the Supplier is first owner; or
- (b) bound by a **signed written assignment** to the Supplier of their rights in the Work, existing and
  future, not conditional on payment.

2.2 The Supplier will give Gridsmith, on request, a list of those individuals and copies or
confirmations of the documents in 2.1(b).

2.3 The Supplier will not use any sub-supplier without Gridsmith's written consent and without imposing
the same obligations.

### 3. Moral rights

3.1 The Supplier will procure, **before the Work is delivered**, a waiver in the form of template 05,
signed by **each individual author** of the Work, in favour of Gridsmith, its licensees and successors
in title, and its clients. For a ghostwritten book, each waiver covers both the section 77 and section
80 rights, with no assertion.

3.2 The Supplier itself does not assert and will procure that none of its personnel asserts the section
77 right in the Work, except as a credit agreed in writing by Gridsmith.

## Notes

**A — why both 2 and 3.** Clause 2 gets the copyright into the Supplier so that clause 1 can assign it.
Clause 3 is separate because moral rights stay with the individual whatever happens to the copyright
(CDPA section 94, **not read at source**).
