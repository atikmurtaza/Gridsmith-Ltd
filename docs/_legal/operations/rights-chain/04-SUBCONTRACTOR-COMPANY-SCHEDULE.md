# Template 04 — Rights and confidentiality schedule: subcontracting company

**Status:** internal template (`GS-LEGAL-001-R4`, 7 October 2026). **Not signed.** Not legal advice.
Fill-ins are `[● …]`.

**Use:** attach to the services agreement with a **company or other organisation** that supplies work
through its own people. Sign it **before any of their work is used**. A company cannot waive its
individuals' moral rights for them (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 ("CDPA") section 87(2)), so
this schedule also obliges it to deliver the Annex A waiver (template 05) from each individual
author. Every other term is written out in full, because the Supplier never receives the other
templates; only Annex A is attached.

## Schedule — intellectual property and confidentiality

**Parties:** Gridsmith Ltd, company number 17050842 (**"Gridsmith"**), and [● company name], company
number [●], of [● registered office] (**"the Supplier"**).
**Engagement:** [● project reference / description].

### 1. Assignment

1.1 The Supplier **assigns to Gridsmith**, with full title guarantee, all copyright, design right,
database right and other intellectual property rights, in every country, in everything created for the
Engagement by the Supplier or its personnel (the **"Work"**): existing rights now, and future rights by
way of **present assignment of future rights**, so that each vests in Gridsmith on creation (CDPA
sections 90(3) and 91).

1.2 **Not conditional on payment.**

1.3 **Pre-existing material.** Material the Supplier or its personnel created before the Engagement and
include in the Work (listed in Part A) is **[● assigned with the Work / licensed to Gridsmith on a
perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable and sub-licensable basis, so that Gridsmith
can pass it to its client with the deliverable]**. Nothing not listed may be included.

1.4 **Consideration.** The fees payable under the Engagement are the consideration for this Schedule.
[● Owner or adviser: whether a further nominal sum is recited is a choice; whether section 91 needs
consideration is **UNVERIFIED** (`README.md`).]

### 2. Chain of title

2.1 The Supplier warrants that, before any of them works on the Engagement, every individual who
contributes to the Work is either:
- (a) its **employee**, working in the course of employment, so that the Supplier is first owner; or
- (b) bound by a **signed written assignment** to the Supplier of their rights in the Work, existing and
  future, not conditional on payment.

2.2 The Supplier will give Gridsmith, on request, a list of those individuals and copies or
confirmations of the documents in 2.1(b).

2.3 The Supplier will not use any sub-supplier without Gridsmith's written consent and without imposing
the same obligations.

### 3. Moral rights

3.1 The Supplier will procure, **before the Work is delivered**, a waiver in the form attached as
**Annex A**, signed by **each individual author** of the Work, in favour of Gridsmith, its licensees and successors
in title, and its clients. For a ghostwritten book, each waiver covers both the section 77 and section
80 rights, with no assertion.

3.2 The Supplier itself does not assert and will procure that none of its personnel asserts the section
77 right in the Work, except as a credit agreed in writing by Gridsmith.

### 4. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that:
- (a) the Work is original to the Supplier's personnel, except for third-party material listed in Part B;
- (b) the Work does not, so far as the Supplier knows after reasonable care, infringe anyone else's
  rights, and contains nothing the Supplier knows to be defamatory or unlawful;
- (c) neither the Supplier nor its personnel has granted or will grant any right in the Work to anyone
  else; and
- (d) every generative AI tool used for the Work is listed in Part C, and the Supplier has checked that
  its terms allow the output to be assigned or used commercially as this Schedule requires.

### 4A. AI tools and client material

The Supplier and its personnel will not put client material or Gridsmith confidential information into
any AI tool that uses inputs to train its models, and will use only tools listed in Part C or approved
in writing by Gridsmith.

### 4B. Confidentiality

The Supplier and its personnel keep confidential all client material, project information and Gridsmith
information, during and after the Engagement, and use it only for the Engagement. They will not show the
Work in a portfolio or name the client without Gridsmith's written consent, which Gridsmith gives only
with the client's written consent. For a ghostwritten book, they will not disclose that Gridsmith, the
Supplier or any of its personnel worked on it unless the client agrees in writing.

### 4C. Further assurance

The Supplier will sign, and procure that its personnel sign, any further document reasonably needed to
give effect to this Schedule, at Gridsmith's cost.

### 4D. Responsibility for personnel

The Supplier is responsible for its personnel's compliance with this Schedule.

### 5. Indemnity

The Supplier will indemnify Gridsmith against losses arising from a breach of clause 2, 3 or 4.

## Notes

**A — why both 2 and 3.** Clause 2 gets the copyright into the Supplier so that clause 1 can assign it.
Clause 3 is separate because moral rights stay with the individual whatever happens to the copyright
(CDPA section 94, **not read at source**).
