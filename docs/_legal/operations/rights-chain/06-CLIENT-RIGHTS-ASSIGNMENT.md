# Template 06 — Client rights-assignment instrument

**Status:** internal template (`GS-LEGAL-001-R4`, 7 October 2026). **Not signed; none has been issued.**
Not legal advice. Fill-ins are `[● …]`.

**Use:** the document the adopted client terms promise once the client has paid in full:
- Consumers 3.1 §13 ¶2: "a short written confirmation of the transfer, signed on behalf of Gridsmith Ltd";
- Business Clients 3.1 §9.3 ¶1: "a short document signed, including electronically, on behalf of
  Gridsmith Ltd".

It uses **operative words of assignment** ("assigns", "transfers"), not only "confirms", so that it is
itself the signed writing that Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 ("CDPA") section 90(3) requires
even if the contract's own wording is ever read as a promise to assign rather than an assignment.

**It must not give more than the contract.** The wording below follows each contract's own words for
what is transferred, what is excluded, and the background licence (non-negotiable 6). Do not add
warranties, guarantees or extra rights here.

**Before signing, check:**
1. The client has paid all sums due for the deliverables listed (Cons 13 ¶2; Bus 9.3 ¶1).
2. Every contributor's template 01, 02, 03 or 04 was signed **before** their work was used, and is in the
   title register (07). **Gridsmith cannot assign what it does not hold.**
3. For a ghostwritten book, each writer's template 05 is signed, unless the quotation or Scope says
   otherwise (Cons 10.2; Bus 13).
4. Any deliverable the quotation says is **licensed** rather than transferred (consumer) is left out of
   the Schedule.
5. Third-party and background material is identified.
6. Any AI-tool use was disclosed in the quotation, Scope or on delivery (the generative-AI paragraph of Cons 13 and of Bus 9.3).

Sign as **simple signed writing, not a deed**. Send it as a PDF with an electronic signature or a scanned
handwritten one. Enter it in the title register (07) and keep it under retention row R7.

## Notes

**A — not a deed.** A deed would carry a 12-year limitation period (Limitation Act 1980 section 8, search
summary only). Section 90(3) needs only writing signed on behalf of the assignor.

**B — electronic signature.** Satisfies section 90(3), medium-high confidence (B, citing the Law
Commission and Electronic Communications Act 2000 section 7).

**C — partial payment.** Issue it only for deliverables paid in full. A later instrument covers later
stages.

**D — design right and database right formalities** are **UNVERIFIED** (`README.md`).
