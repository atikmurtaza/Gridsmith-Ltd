# Template 02 — Employee intellectual property and confidentiality clause

**Status:** internal template (`GS-LEGAL-001-R4`, 7 October 2026). **Not signed; nobody is recorded as
employed.** Not legal advice. Fill-ins are `[● …]`.

**Use:** a clause to insert into a written contract of employment between Gridsmith Ltd and an employee,
**only if** someone is employed under a contract of service. It applies to the owner only if the owner
actually holds such a contract (`README.md`, *The owner employment question*). It does **not** replace
template 01 for the owner: work made before 24 February 2026 or outside employment is still the owner's
until assigned.

It is not a complete employment contract. Other employment terms are outside this pack.

---

## [●] Intellectual property and confidentiality

**[●].1 Work in the course of employment.** The Employee acknowledges that copyright, design right and
database right in every work, design and database the Employee makes in the course of employment belong
to the Company on creation (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 ("CDPA") section 11(2); see note B
for design and database right).

**[●].2 Related work outside the course of employment.** So far as any right in a work, design or
database that relates to the Company's business, or is made using its information, equipment or time,
does not vest in the Company under clause [●].1, the Employee **assigns** it to the Company: existing
rights now, and future rights by way of present assignment of future rights, so that each vests in the
Company on creation (CDPA sections 90(3) and 91). The assignment is not conditional on any payment.
[● Optional: work unrelated to the Company's business, made in the Employee's own time with the
Employee's own equipment and without the Company's confidential information, is excluded.]

**[●].3 AI tools.** The Employee will use for Company work only the AI and software tools on the
Company's approved list (template 07), will not put client confidential material into any tool that uses
inputs to train its models without the client's agreement, and will record in the project file when a
generative AI tool contributed to a deliverable. Any right in tool output made or arranged by the
Employee for the Company is within clauses [●].1 and [●].2.

**[●].4 Moral rights.** The Employee **unconditionally and irrevocably waives** the rights under CDPA
sections 77 and 80 in every work within clauses [●].1 and [●].2, existing and future, in favour of the
Company, its licensees and successors in title, and every client to whom rights in the work are
transferred or licensed (CDPA section 87). For in-course work these rights are already limited
(sections 79(3) and 82; section 82 **search summary only**); the waiver covers the edges.

**[●].5 Credit.** Any credit is given only where the project's quotation or Scope provides for it. For
ghostwriting, the Employee will not disclose that the Company or the Employee worked on a book without
the client's agreement.

**[●].6 Third-party material.** The Employee will not include third-party material in a deliverable
except under a licence recorded in the project file, and will flag any open-source licence that imposes
obligations on the client.

**[●].7 Confidentiality.** During and after employment, the Employee will keep confidential the
Company's and its clients' confidential information, including client material, project details and,
for ghostwriting, the fact of the Company's involvement, and will use it only for the Company's
business.

**[●].8 Further assurance.** During and after employment, the Employee will sign any document reasonably
needed to confirm the Company's title, at the Company's cost.

---

## Notes

**A — whether the waiver is needed.** CDPA section 79(3) disapplies the section 77 right for anything
done with the copyright owner's authority where copyright first vested in the employer, and section 82
qualifies the section 80 right. The waiver is belt and braces and supports Cons 10.2 / Bus 13.

**B — design right and database right.** The employer-first-owner rules for design right and database
right are **UNVERIFIED** in this repository; clause [●].2 is drafted to catch anything that does not
vest on creation.

**C — signature.** The contract containing this clause must be signed by the Employee, because clause
[●].2 is an assignment (section 90(3)) and clause [●].4 a waiver (section 87).
