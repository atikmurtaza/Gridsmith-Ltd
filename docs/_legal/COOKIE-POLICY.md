# Cookie Policy — DRAFT for solicitor review

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective from:** `[TK]` · **Status: DRAFT**

Drafted against PECR 2003 as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

---

## 1. Our position

We place **no non-essential cookie, script or tracking technology until you have made a choice**. Analytics and product-measurement tools are not loaded and suppressed — they are not loaded at all until consent is given.

Accepting and rejecting are equally easy. Both are one click, in identically sized buttons, side by side. Rejecting does not reduce what you can do on this site.

## 2. Strictly necessary — no consent required

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `gs_consent` | Records your cookie choice so we do not ask again | 12 months |
| `__Host-session` | Maintains your session while completing a form | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Protects forms against cross-site request forgery | Session |

These are exempt under PECR because they are strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested. They cannot be switched off, and none of them tracks you across other websites.

## 3. Functional — consent required

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `gs_design_track` | Remembers whether you were looking at brand work or technical work in Gridsmith Design, so we can show you the relevant content first | 90 days |

Not strictly necessary — the site works fully without it. It contains a preference, not an identifier tied to you personally.

## 4. Analytics — consent required

| Provider | Cookies | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics 4 | `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Which pages are used, where visitors arrive from | 24 months |
| PostHog | `ph_*` | Conversion funnels and, where enabled, session replay | 12 months |

**Note on the 2026 exemptions.** The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 introduced a narrow PECR exemption for analytics used solely to produce aggregate statistics. We have chosen not to rely on it. Google Analytics involves a third party and supports non-aggregate use, and session replay is plainly not aggregate statistics. We therefore ask for consent. If that position changes following legal advice, this policy will be updated and re-versioned before any change takes effect.

## 5. What we do not use

- No advertising or retargeting cookies
- No cross-site tracking
- No social media pixels
- No fingerprinting
- No third-party consent platform (our banner is our own code, served from our own domain)

## 6. Changing your mind

Use the **Cookie preferences** link in the footer of any page. Changes take effect immediately. Withdrawing consent stops further collection; it does not delete data already collected — for that, see the Privacy Policy.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent forms from working.

## 7. How we record consent

We keep a record showing that consent was given or refused: a random consent identifier, your choice, which categories you selected, and the version of this policy in force. **No personal data is stored in this record** — the identifier is random and is not linked to your name, email or IP address. We keep it for 24 months.
