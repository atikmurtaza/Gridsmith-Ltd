## 3. Focused existing-terms and Article 28 conclusion

Primary sources checked on 8 October 2026; no broad research programme restarted:

1. [UK GDPR Article 28(3), (4), (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/28/data.html):
   a processor must be bound with regard to its controller by written terms, including electronic
   terms. Sub-processing duties apply when an actual processor engages another processor.
2. [ICO: controllers and processors](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/controllers-and-processors/controllers-and-processors/what-are-controllers-and-processors/):
   employees acting within their duties are part of their controller. Internal authorised staff
   are not automatically a separate processor. Article 29 instructions and Article 32 access and
   confidentiality duties still apply.
3. [ICO: when a contract is needed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/when-is-a-contract-needed-and-why-is-it-important/):
   coverage may use existing direct or other binding contractual arrangements; no bespoke document
   or controller-named purchaser is prescribed. ICO flags its guidance as under review following
   the Data (Use and Access) Act; the cited Article 28 requirements remain in the current statute.
4. [Hostinger Terms](https://www.hostinger.com/legal/universal-terms-of-service-agreement), revised
   29 September 2026: §1 defines Customer to include an entity accessing or using services and makes
   the terms effective on use or electronic acceptance. §2 incorporates the applicable DPA. §3
   addresses an authorised corporate representative acting for the entity. §4 allocates account
   ownership/control; it does not expressly exclude corporate service users from §1 or the DPA.
5. [Hostinger DPA](https://www.hostinger.com/legal/dpa), revised 29 September 2026: the preamble
   supplements the Terms; §1.1 imports undefined capitalised Terms definitions, linking Customer to
   the Terms' corporate-user definition; §1 includes both hosting and email; §2 treats Customer as controller or
   processor as applicable. §7.4 sends incident notices to Customer administrators. §9 sets out
   transfer safeguards including the UK Addendum. It is already incorporated, not a document that
   requires execution of Gridsmith's R10 draft.

**Contractual reading on the established facts:** Gridsmith uses the hosting/email services through
its owner-authorised internal administration. The existing Terms §§1–3 and incorporated DPA provide
the documented direct corporate-user basis for Hostinger's processing with regard to Gridsmith.
R9/R10 treated §4 account ownership as displacing that basis and presumed an external processor;
neither follows from the clarified facts or an express exclusion in the terms. A historical purchase
does not itself create an additional processor or an international transfer to that purchaser.
