## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Gridsmith Ltd is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Our contact details are in section 15.

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you:

- visit gridsmith.uk;
- contact us through a website form, by email, or by WhatsApp or text message;
- ask for a quotation;
- become a client;
- communicate with us about a project;
- raise a complaint; or
- wrote a client review that we show on our website (see section 2A).

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

- your name;
- email address;
- telephone number where provided;
- company or organisation;
- the Gridsmith studio or service you are interested in;
- the details you give in an enquiry, such as your project requirements, rough budget and timeline and, for publishing enquiries, answers about your book or content (for example its stage, length, genre and purpose) and any link you choose to share to a manuscript or other material;
- technical records of each form submission, such as when it was made, a random reference and a coded fingerprint of the submission that we use to recognise and ignore accidental duplicate submissions;
- correspondence and project communications;
- quotation, contract, invoice and payment records;
- project materials you choose to provide after contact; and
- technical information about each request to the website, such as your IP address and browser details, which our hosting provider processes in operating the website.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as health, religious or political details. Please do not include it in an enquiry or in material you link to unless it is needed for the work you are asking about. If you do, we will use it only for that purpose.

Our enquiry database does not store your IP address or browser details. Our providers' own systems do record them: when you submit a form, it is received by Supabase, whose logs record your IP address, approximate country and browser details, and our hosting provider records similar technical information about each request to the website. Sections 6 and 8 say more.

## 2A. Reviews shown on our website

Our website may show reviews that clients left for us on Freelancer.com. We show the review text and rating only, without the reviewer's name or other details, and link to our Freelancer profile, where the original review appears. We do this in our legitimate interest in showing prospective clients genuine feedback. If you wrote one of these reviews and would like it removed from our website, email contact@gridsmith.uk and we will remove it.

## 7. International transfers

Enquiries submitted through our forms are stored in Ireland, and our website is hosted in France. The UK recognises both countries as providing adequate protection for personal data. Supabase, which provides our enquiry database, is a Singapore company and uses sub-processors that include US companies.

The servers that receive a form submission run close to the person sending it, so a submission made from outside the UK or Europe may be processed briefly in that region before it is stored. Our email-notification provider, Resend, processes the notification and keeps its delivery records in the United States. Our hosting provider's content delivery network may serve the website from locations outside the UK and Europe.

Where personal data is transferred to a country that does not have UK adequacy regulations, we rely on the safeguards in each provider's data processing terms: for Supabase, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses; for Resend and Hostinger, the international transfer safeguards set out in their data processing agreements. You can ask us for details of these safeguards at contact@gridsmith.uk.

## 8. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of:

- whether an enquiry became a client project;
- whether follow-up is reasonably expected;
- contractual and warranty requirements;
- accounting and tax obligations;
- the time within which legal claims can be brought; and
- the need to resolve complaints.

Our retention periods are:

- enquiries that do not lead to a project, and the internal email that tells us about them: 12 months from when we received them or from our last contact with you;
- enquiries we identify as spam: 30 days;
- if your enquiry leads to a project, the project and contract records: six years after the end of the financial year in which the project ends, because of tax law and the time within which legal claims can be brought;
- documents that prove who owns work we created, such as signed transfers and waivers: for as long as those rights are relied on, and for six years after that;
- complaints: with the project record or, if there is none, two years after the complaint is closed; and
- project working files: returned or deleted within 90 days after the project ends unless we agree otherwise with you. We keep the final delivered version with the project record.

Records are deleted, or made anonymous, at the first of our monthly reviews after their period ends.

Our providers keep some records for their own periods, and deleting an enquiry does not shorten them. These are Supabase's request logs and any backup data (section 6), Resend's notification records (30 days, with backups kept for 7 days), and Hostinger's technical, security and mailbox records, which Hostinger keeps for periods it determines according to its operational, security and legal requirements.

Accounting records are kept for six years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as tax law requires, and for longer where tax law or an open matter requires it.
