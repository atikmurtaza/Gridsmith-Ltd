## 1. Who we are

Gridsmith Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through gridsmith.co.uk. We operate three trading divisions — Gridsmith Design, Gridsmith Digital and Gridsmith Press. All three are part of the same company, and personal data is held once by Gridsmith Ltd rather than separately by each division.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Data | When |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry data | Name, email, company, role, phone, message, budget band, timeline, project details | When you submit an enquiry, estimate, sample request or assessment request |
| Interaction data | Pages viewed, referrer, device type, approximate location from IP | Only where analytics consent has been given |
| Consent record | A random consent identifier, your choice, the categories selected, the policy version | When you respond to the cookie banner |
| Communication data | Emails and messages between us | Throughout an enquiry or engagement |
| Client data | Contract, project and billing information | If you become a client |

**We do not collect special category data**, and we ask that you do not include it in free-text fields.

**We do not accept file uploads through this website.** Where a brief, manuscript or drawing needs to be shared, we ask for a link. This is deliberate — it avoids us holding your intellectual property before there is a contract governing it.

## 3. Lawful bases

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Responding to an enquiry | Legitimate interests (responding to a request you made), and steps prior to entering a contract |
| Providing services under a contract | Performance of a contract |
| Sending you a sample pack, estimate or assessment you requested | Legitimate interests |
| Marketing emails to existing clients about similar services | Legitimate interests + PECR soft opt-in, with an unsubscribe in every message |
| Marketing emails to anyone else | Consent |
| Analytics and performance measurement | Consent |
| Accounting and tax records | Legal obligation |
| Establishing or defending legal claims | Legitimate interests |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have assessed that our interest in operating and improving a professional services business does not override your rights. You may object at any time — see §10.

## 4. How we use it

- To respond to enquiries and provide the response we have committed to
- To prepare scopes, estimates and proposals
- To deliver services and support
- To send requested materials
- To improve the website (analytics, with consent)
- To keep accounting records
- To meet legal obligations

**We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to train any model.**

## 5. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means.

Our website includes estimating tools (project estimator, drawing estimator, publishing path finder). These produce **indicative guidance only**. They do not decide whether we will work with you, and every engagement follows a human conversation. Their output is not a quote and does not bind either party.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to those who need it. The enquiry database uses row-level security so that the public website can write enquiries but cannot read them. We do not store payment card details — payments are handled by our payment provider.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to: be informed · access · rectification · erasure · restrict processing · data portability · object · not be subject to solely automated decision-making · withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact `[TK email]`. We will respond within one month, and will tell you promptly if we need longer because the request is complex.

There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## 10. Objecting to legitimate interests

If we rely on legitimate interests, you can object. For direct marketing, we will stop immediately and without question. For other purposes, we will stop unless we can demonstrate compelling grounds that override your rights.

## 11. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy. In short: nothing non-essential is placed until you have made a choice, and you can change that choice at any time from the link in the footer.

## 12. Complaints

**Data protection complaints procedure** *(required from 19 June 2026)*

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data:

1. Email `[TK email]` with "Data protection complaint" in the subject.
2. We will acknowledge within **5 working days**.
3. We will investigate and respond substantively within **30 days**. If we need longer, we will tell you why and when to expect a response.
4. Our response will explain what we found, what we have done, and what you can do next.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at any time, and you do not have to come to us first. ICO: ico.org.uk · 0303 123 1113.

We will post any changes here with an updated version number and effective date. Material changes will be notified to clients directly.
