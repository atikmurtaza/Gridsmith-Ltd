## Notes

**A — consideration.** Section 90(3) requires only signed writing. Whether a present assignment of
future copyright under section 91 needs consideration is **UNVERIFIED** in this repository (it speaks
of an "agreement"). A nominal consideration is conservative drafting, not a requirement established
here. The owner, or an adviser if the owner chooses to take advice, decides.

**B — design right and database right.** Design right assignment (CDPA section 222(3)) and prospective
ownership (section 223), and database right formalities (Copyright and Rights in Databases Regulations
1997), were **not read at source**. The wording above is written to cover them, but whether it meets
their formalities is **UNVERIFIED**.

**C — not a deed.** A deed would carry a 12-year limitation period (Limitation Act 1980 section 8,
search summary only). Signed writing is enough for section 90(3) and is what this template uses. The
heading says so deliberately.

**D — false attribution.** Section 84 is not waived because it protects the owner against being named
as author of work they did not write. It does not stop a client publishing under the client's own name.
