# Template 03 — Rights and confidentiality schedule: individual subcontractor

**Status:** internal template (`GS-LEGAL-001-R4`, 7 October 2026). **Not signed.** Not legal advice.
Fill-ins are `[● …]`.

**Use:** attach to the services agreement or written engagement with an **individual** freelancer
(including a commissioned writer or a ghostwriter). It must be **signed before any of their work is
used** in a client deliverable (Cons 13 ¶4; Bus 9.3 ¶3). Add the writer addendum or the ghostwriter
addendum where it applies. Collect template 05 as well where the project needs a stand-alone waiver.

The fee, timetable, payment and other commercial terms are in the engagement itself and are not set
here.

## Schedule — intellectual property and confidentiality

**Parties:** Gridsmith Ltd, company number 17050842 (**"Gridsmith"**), and [● full name] of
[● address] (**"the Contributor"**).
**Engagement:** [● project reference / description; or "all engagements between the parties from the
date of this Schedule"].

### 1. Assignment

1.1 The Contributor **assigns to Gridsmith**, with full title guarantee, all copyright, design right,
database right and other intellectual property rights, in every country, in everything the Contributor
creates for the Engagement (the **"Work"**):
- (a) as to existing rights, now; and
- (b) as to future rights, by way of **present assignment of future rights**, so that each right vests
  in Gridsmith **on creation** (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 ("CDPA") sections 90(3) and 91).

1.2 The assignment **is not conditional on payment.** If Gridsmith does not pay, the Contributor's
remedy is a claim for the fee, not the return of the rights.

1.3 **Pre-existing material.** Material the Contributor created before the Engagement and includes in
the Work (listed in Part A below) is **[● assigned with the Work / licensed to Gridsmith on a
perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable and sub-licensable basis, so that
Gridsmith can pass it to its client with the deliverable]**. Nothing not listed may be included.

### 2. Moral rights

2.1 The Contributor **unconditionally and irrevocably waives** the rights under CDPA sections 77 and 80
in the Work, in favour of Gridsmith, its licensees and successors in title, and every client to whom
rights in the Work are transferred or licensed, and their licensees and successors in title (CDPA
section 87). [● Writer addendum varies this for a credited writer.]

2.2 The Contributor has not asserted and will not assert the section 77 right (section 78), except as
the writer addendum allows.

### 3. Warranties

The Contributor warrants that:
- (a) the Work is the Contributor's original work, except for third-party material listed in Part B;
- (b) the Work does not, so far as the Contributor knows after reasonable care, infringe anyone else's
  rights, and contains nothing the Contributor knows to be defamatory or unlawful;
- (c) the Contributor has not granted and will not grant any right in the Work to anyone else; and
- (d) every generative AI tool used for the Work is listed in Part C, and the Contributor has checked
  that its terms allow the output to be assigned or used commercially as this Schedule requires.

### 4. AI tools and client material

The Contributor will not put client material or Gridsmith confidential information into any AI tool that
uses inputs to train its models, and will use only tools listed in Part C or approved in writing by
Gridsmith.

### 5. Confidentiality

The Contributor keeps confidential all client material, project information and Gridsmith information,
during and after the Engagement, and uses it only for the Engagement. **The Contributor will not show
the Work in a portfolio or name the client** without Gridsmith's written consent, which Gridsmith gives
only with the client's written consent (Cons 14; Bus 9.4).

### 6. Further assurance

The Contributor will sign any further document, including template 05, reasonably needed to give effect
to this Schedule, at Gridsmith's cost.

### 7. Law

This Schedule is governed by the law of England and Wales.

## Addendum W — commissioned writer (credited)

W1. The client's quotation or Scope records the credit: [● e.g. "Text by [name]" / "with [name]"].

W2. Clause 2 is varied: the Contributor **asserts** the section 77 right only so far as needed to receive
that credit, in the form and places stated, and waives it otherwise. The section 80 waiver in clause 2.1
is unaffected.

W3. Where the client's quotation or Scope requires a full waiver, W2 does not apply.

## Addendum G — ghostwriter

G1. The Contributor **waives both** the section 77 and section 80 rights, unconditionally, as in clause
2.1, and **will not assert** the section 77 right anywhere (no credit line, no statement of authorship,
no portfolio or social-media mention) unless the client agrees in writing.

G2. The Contributor will not disclose to anyone that Gridsmith or the Contributor worked on the book,
unless the client agrees in writing (Cons 10.2, 14; Bus 13). This survives the end of the Engagement.

G3. The Contributor accepts that the client may publish the book under the client's own name and may
edit it freely.

G4. The Contributor signs template 05 for the book, naming it, in addition to this Schedule.

## Notes

**A — before use.** "Before their work is used" (Cons 13 ¶4; Bus 9.3 ¶3) means this Schedule is signed
before any of the Work goes into a client deliverable. Record the signature date in template 07.

**B — payment conditionality.** A payment-conditional assignment is doubtful under section 91's
"entitled as against all other persons" test (`../RIGHTS-CHAIN.md` §1, B, medium). Clause 1.2 is
therefore deliberate.

**C — design right and database right formalities** are **UNVERIFIED** (`README.md`).

**D — consideration.** The engagement fee is the consideration. Whether section 91 strictly needs
consideration is **UNVERIFIED**.
