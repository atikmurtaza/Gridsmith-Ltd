# Analytics and AI activation record

22 October 2026. The notices are OWNER_ADOPTED and unpublished. Internal controls moved out of the public notices; no activation or implementation authority. Privacy 2.6 and Cookies 2.4 describe future processing. Approval of those texts alone cannot support active analytics. Notice-only launch remains permitted.

- GA4 and PostHog remain disabled until separate authority, updated accurate public notices and verified `ANALYTICS-READY` evidence. An approval date is not activation evidence. Publish actual cookie/data/provider/retention/transfer information before collecting events, and reset adoption on changed text.
- Planned collection: sanitised page paths without queries/fragments, division, contact-link categories, form starts, outcomes, bounded error categories and outbound-link categories. No form text, contact details, manuscripts or confidential project files in events. Provider receipt of IP addresses must be disclosed accurately even if later discarded. Replay, heatmaps, automatic page-text capture, advertising personalisation and Google Signals remain outside scope.
- Require affirmative current-version consent before requests to analytics providers, equally prominent accept/reject, working forms on refusal, successful evidence recording before a grant activates collection, immediate refusal/withdrawal without waiting for a write, and rejection of legacy/malformed/expired consent. Prepared choice lifetime is 180 days; cookie notice acknowledgement is 365 days and never authorises analytics. The separately prepared version 2.4 rejects 2.3 and older choices; this primary branch runs notice-only acknowledgement, with no analytics choice runtime.
- Planned cookies: versioned `gs_consent` choice/reference, Google `_ga`/`_ga_[ID]`, and Gridsmith `gs_aid` for PostHog. Analytics identifiers expire with the choice, at most 180 days, without activity renewal. Verify actual settings and network/storage behaviour before offering them.
- Consent table evidence is a random reference, choice, policy version, site and time, without name/email/IP/user-agent/analytics ID fields. Provider request logs remain separate personal-data processing. Prepared evidence expiry is 18 months from each event, including withdrawal. Keep only proportionate evidence with a lawful basis.
- Retention and erasure: apply RETENTION-SCHEDULE.md and DELETION-IMPLEMENTATION.md. Preview daily job configuration and rollback-only manual proof do not establish successful daily operation or Production deployment. The last readback records no genuine runs; cause remains unknown. Do not mark this satisfied without actual environment-specific evidence. No production mutation is authorised here.
- Verify Google two-month user/event retention and periodic deletion, with aggregate reports distinguished. PostHog Free's one-year query window does not prove physical deletion. Establish actual account-specific retention/deletion for every relevant copy. Withdrawal stops further collection and requires assessing the lawful basis for existing personal data and erasure where required, not merely cookie removal. Demonstrate provider rights handling without inventing a universal erasure service.
- Verify exact provider entities, terms/DPAs, processing and access locations, applicable transfer instruments and supplementary safeguards. EU storage alone is insufficient. UK DPF Extension requires covered active certification and a covered transfer. Keep processing and onward-transfer records.
- AI-assisted analytics: default to aggregates checked for identifiability, for human-reviewed trends and improvements. Pseudonymous exports remain personal data where identifiable. Before any external AI personal-data processing, identify purpose, data, lawful basis, provider, locations, retention, training use and access; complete contracts/transfers, DPIA screening, rights controls, accurate notices and any required consent. Analytics consent is not blanket permission for an unidentified AI service. No confidential client material used for model training without client agreement and lawful arrangements. No solely automated significant decisions are proposed.

These are existing controls to evidence if activation is later requested, not a requirement to activate analytics or start another development phase before owner adoption of the seven documents.
