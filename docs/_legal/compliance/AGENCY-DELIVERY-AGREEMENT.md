# Agency delivery agreement — execution template

**Status: unexecuted template, prepared 22 October 2026.** This is an internal agreement for an actual agency, not an additional public policy or evidence of existing signatures. Complete a project order identifying the agency's exact legal entity, registered address, signatories, delivery/access countries, work, fees, deadlines and authorised people. Each party signs the order incorporating these terms. Do not give access before the processing and transfer schedules are complete and approved. Existing contracts may satisfy these requirements; check them before duplicating agreements.

## 1. Project and responsibility

Gridsmith Ltd, company 17050842, registered office 30 Briarfield Road, Farnworth, Bolton, BL4 0HD (Gridsmith), appoints the agency identified in the signed order to perform only the services and deliverables specified there. The agency shall use reasonable care and skill, comply with the agreed brief, meet the agreed timetable, promptly report risks or defects and correct non-conforming work at its own cost within a reasonable agreed time. Changes to scope, fees, staffing or delivery/access locations require Gridsmith's prior written approval. The order fixes payment and acceptance; silence is not approval of additional charges.

Gridsmith remains the client's contracting service provider. The agency cannot bind Gridsmith or contact a client in Gridsmith's name without written authority. It shall not delegate work or allow another business or unapproved individual access without prior written authorisation and equivalent binding protections. Gridsmith's authorisation does not remove the agency's responsibility for its personnel or approved suppliers.

## 2. Confidentiality and security

The agency shall use client and Gridsmith information only to perform the order, disclose it only to authorised people who need it and are bound by confidentiality, protect it with appropriate security and return or delete it as instructed. This covers manuscripts, authorship/ghostwriting involvement, briefs, designs, source code, credentials, client identities and communications. It continues after the order ends; trade secrets, unpublished client work and personal data remain protected for as long as confidentiality or law requires. Exceptions are information lawfully public without breach, already lawfully known, independently developed or disclosure required by law; legally permitted advance notice must be given of compulsory disclosure.

No portfolio use, public credit, case study, client naming, review request, resale or marketing use is permitted without Gridsmith's prior written approval supported by the client's permission. No confidential material or personal data may be uploaded to an AI service or used for model training without prior documented authorisation and the required lawful arrangements. An ordinary consumer AI account is not assumed safe.

Use individual accounts, MFA where available, least privilege, encryption in transit and at rest where appropriate, controlled devices and timely security updates. Do not share passwords, use public links, keep unnecessary local copies or transfer files by an unapproved channel. Report an actual or suspected security incident to contact@gridsmith.uk without undue delay, with an initial notification within 24 hours after awareness and updates as facts emerge. That contractual target does not postpone an earlier legal duty. Preserve relevant evidence and assist containment without unauthorised access or disclosure.

## 3. Intellectual property and client ownership

The client keeps its supplied material. The agency receives only a limited licence to use it for the order and cannot assert a lien over client data or credentials. Subject to separately identified background and third-party material, the agency hereby assigns to Gridsmith all copyright and other assignable intellectual-property rights it owns in the commissioned deliverables, including future rights to the extent the law permits, worldwide for the full term and renewals, with the right to transfer them to Gridsmith's client. The agreed fees include consideration for that assignment; payment duties remain enforceable separately. The agency shall sign further documents reasonably needed to perfect title.

Before use, the agency shall obtain rights from each employee, freelancer or other creator sufficient to give Gridsmith and its client the agreed rights. It shall provide evidence on reasonable request. It shall obtain signed moral-rights waivers from the people who hold those rights where permitted and needed for the agreed attribution, adaptation and exploitation; where waiver is unavailable, obtain adequate legally effective consent and disclose the limit before use. The agency's signature alone cannot waive another person's rights. Actual creator jurisdictions and restrictions must be assessed.

The order must identify any retained background material or third-party/open-source components, applicable licences and restrictions before inclusion. For the agency's own background material necessarily embedded in the deliverable, the agency grants Gridsmith and its client a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence to use, reproduce, modify, maintain and exploit it with the deliverable and authorise their contractors to do so. Do not include material whose licence prevents the promised client use. Disclose AI-generated components and their provenance/rights limits; do not guarantee exclusive copyright that does not exist.

## 4. Personal data — role and instructions

Complete the order's processing schedule before access: controller; whether Gridsmith is controller or processor; agency role; subject matter, duration, nature and purpose; data and data-subject categories; systems and locations; authorised workers; sub-processors; security; assistance contacts; retention/return/deletion; and international-transfer route.

Where the agency processes personal data for Gridsmith as controller, it acts only as processor. Where Gridsmith processes for a client controller, the agency acts as sub-processor and access requires the client's prior specific or general written authorisation under the client agreement. The agency shall process only documented lawful instructions, including on transfers; ensure confidentiality; apply UK GDPR Article 32 security; inform Gridsmith immediately if an instruction appears unlawful; and give legally permitted advance notice of a legal requirement to process otherwise.

The agency shall not appoint another processor without written authorisation. For a generally authorised change, give at least 14 days' advance information for objection and do not provide access while an objection remains unresolved. Impose equivalent obligations on authorised onward processors and remain responsible for their performance. No approval under this clause dispenses with the client controller's authorisation where required.

The agency shall assist Gridsmith and the controller with data-subject requests, security, breach assessment/notifications, DPIAs and regulator consultation as required by Article 28, taking account of the processing and available information. Forward rights requests promptly; do not respond substantively on the controller's behalf without instructions except as required by law. Make information demonstrating compliance available and allow and contribute to reasonable audits and inspections by Gridsmith, the controller or their appointed auditor; contractual notice must not obstruct urgent incidents or regulator access.

At the end of processing, at the controller's choice return or delete personal data and delete copies unless law requires retention. Any required retention must be identified, limited and protected from ordinary use. Backups await a documented finite expiry only where selective deletion is not feasible; restoration must reapply deletions. Provide deletion/return confirmation and escalate failures. Do not treat the agency's general preference or convenience as a legal duty to retain.

If the agency determines its own purposes, identify that controller activity separately and agree the lawful disclosure, transparency, responsibilities and transfer route; do not mislabel it as processing on instructions. No joint-controller relationship is assumed; if purposes and essential means are jointly determined, document the arrangement required by Article 26.

## 5. International access

No personal data may be made available from an unapproved country or to an unapproved recipient. Remote access by a separate overseas agency counts even if the server stays in the UK. The signed order must record actual countries and onward access. UK/EEA work is not a licence to use an unapproved offshore supplier.

For a restricted transfer, the parties must establish applicable adequacy or execute the official appropriate instrument and complete the required data protection test and supplementary measures before access. Use the checklist in `INTERNATIONAL-TRANSFERS.md`. An agency's US address, a DPF logo, generic confidentiality clause or this agreement is not by itself a transfer mechanism. The mandatory terms of the official IDTA or applicable SCCs/UK Addendum prevail for transfers if they conflict with the order. No unauthorised alteration to mandatory terms is permitted.

## 6. End of work and law

Gridsmith may suspend access or terminate affected work for material confidentiality, rights or data-protection breach or an unresolved unlawful transfer. The agency shall stop work as instructed, secure and hand over work in progress and cooperate with a safe return/deletion and transition. Properly accrued payment obligations and confidentiality, rights, data-protection and assistance duties survive as needed. Liability, payment disputes and any project-specific cap must be agreed in the signed order with regard to actual risk; no unagreed indemnity or insurance cover is invented by this template.

The order and these terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, subject to the mandatory transfer instrument and applicable law. Jurisdiction and enforceability for the actual overseas parties must be confirmed in the order. Neither signature nor a jurisdiction clause guarantees practical overseas enforcement.

## 7. Execution checklist

The signed order must identify both parties/signatories and date, expressly incorporate these terms and attach the completed work/processing/security/location schedules and any official executed transfer instrument. Record client authorisation where needed and signed creator assignments/waivers. An incomplete template, sent email or unsigned draft is not execution evidence. This template does not retrospectively cure any previous access or rights gap.
