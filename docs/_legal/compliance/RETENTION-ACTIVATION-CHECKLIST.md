## Recurring operation

- Monthly, from the recorded 2 November 2026 cadence: review unconverted enquiries against the 12-month period and last substantive contact; spam against 30 days; associated internal mailbox, sent/trash and WhatsApp/SMS copies against the same purpose. Review obsolete exports and the R7 pre-cleanup baseline when its recorded hold ends. Classify and count before deletion, check exceptions, use only the minimum necessary data, obtain the required system authority, then read back and log the result.
- Quarterly, from 4 January 2027: check project working copies against the 90-day rule, agency return/deletion confirmations, final delivered-set retention and notification/outbox records against the applicable schedule and deployed schema.
- Annually, from 1 March 2027: review contracts, tax/accounting records, title evidence, complaints and any extended hold against the current schedule. Record the reason and next review date for each justified exception.
- Keep complaints, disputes, rights requests, statutory holds and necessary title evidence while their documented purpose requires it. A hold must be specific and reviewed; it does not justify retaining every working copy.

Do not reuse the old row-code SQL or one-off cleanup against a changed database. Follow `DELETION-IMPLEMENTATION.md` for selection, notification linkage, agency/provider copies, backups, restore reapplication, monitoring and failure handling. Preserve recovery evidence securely and minimise any export. Record date/operator, category, candidate and deleted counts, exceptions, read-back, export expiry and next review without personal content. Stop on unexpected counts or unavailable systems; escalate and log the missed run rather than claiming completion.
