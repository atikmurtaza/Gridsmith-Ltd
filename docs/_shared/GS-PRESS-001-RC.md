## Exact-SHA release receipt and determination

Only after local applicable checks pass may this candidate be committed and pushed to `staging/gs-press-001-press`. Its own CI and protected non-production Preview must both be verified against the final SHA. An older CI run or deployment never accepts a newer commit.

Following the Design RC receipt convention, the final commit cannot contain its own hash. The final release handoff and ignored `node_modules/.cache/gs-press-001-rc/final-release-receipt.json` record the exact final SHA, commits, remote/main checks, CI URL/result, Lighthouse route/device medians, Preview URL/deployment/SHA/protection and runtime results. They determine **PASS** only once the exact-SHA conditions are satisfied; until then the candidate is not accepted. This avoids an endless sequence of evidence-only commits.
