# Professional review of three Technical service descriptions

**Gridsmith Ltd (company 17050842) — Gridsmith Design.** Review pack for `GS-X002`, prepared
2 October 2026 (`GS-PROD-004A`). Self-contained: no knowledge of the Gridsmith programme is needed.

## 1. Purpose

Gridsmith intends to publish three service pages describing a **limited** technical drawing and
documentation service. Before they go live we want a suitably qualified professional — for example a
chartered engineer, or an engineering-drawing or technical-documentation professional of equivalent
standing — to confirm that the public wording describes that limited service accurately and could not
reasonably lead a client to believe Gridsmith takes on professional engineering responsibility.

**In scope:** the public wording reproduced in §3 and §4, read against the intended boundaries in §2.

**Out of scope — please do not assess:** insurance; contract or liability terms (these are with a
solicitor separately); Gridsmith's own competence or qualifications; pricing; any other part of the
website.

## 2. Intended service boundaries

Gridsmith provides, **to the client's brief and from information the client supplies**:

- CAD drafting — models and drawings from sketches, marked-up prints, measurements, scans or a sample;
- preparation of engineering drawings (general arrangement, detail) and schematic diagrams;
- technical documentation — layout, illustration and production of manuals, instructions and
  specification/data sheets whose technical content the client supplies and approves.

Gridsmith does **not** provide, and does not intend to imply:

- engineering design responsibility (design intent, dimensions, tolerances, fitness for purpose stay
  with the client or the client's engineer);
- engineering calculations;
- regulatory or statutory approval;
- certification, including code-compliance certification;
- stamping of drawings;
- professional sign-off, or approval for construction or manufacture;
- a responsible-designer or responsible-engineer role.

**Relevant metadata.** The three records sit in a "Technical" group that the website will not publish
until this review is complete (a flag, `professionalScopeConfirmed`, is set only on the strength of
this review). No standards code, certification body or qualification is named anywhere in the copy.

## 5. Questions — please answer each in writing

For each of the three pages (§3.1–§3.3, with §3.4 and §4 where they apply):

- **A.** Does the description accurately represent a limited CAD drafting, drawing-preparation or
  documentation service, rather than professional engineering design responsibility?
- **B.** Could any wording reasonably imply calculations, certification, approval, sign-off,
  responsible-designer status or another professional engineering responsibility that Gridsmith says it
  does not provide? If so, quote it.
- **C.** Are the exclusions ("What you do not get") and boundaries technically clear and consistent
  with each other and with the rest of the page?
- **D.** Should any specific wording change so that a reasonable client is not misled about the scope?
  Please quote the current words and give the replacement.
- **E.** On the public wording alone, is each page suitable to publish within that limited scope —
  yes, yes subject to the changes in D, or no?

You are not being asked for legal advice or for any view on insurance.
